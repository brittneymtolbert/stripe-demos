Marketing Adoption Coach

An AI coach embedded with a cohort of marketers. It answers "how do I use AI for this?" in the team channel, tracks where each person is on the adoption journey, routes what it can't answer to a human, and turns those answers into new playbooks. Every Friday it briefs leadership.

How it works

1 · Ask in the channel

Marketers ask in plain language. The coach answers from the team's playbook library, tailored to their role.

2 · Track the journey

Every question and win is logged. People move from awareness to first win, regular use, a transformed workflow, and self-sufficiency.

3 · Route to a human

No playbook? The coach says so, flags it for a human, and the human's answer becomes a new playbook the whole team can reuse.

4 · Brief leadership

A weekly summary shows highlights, who needs support, and which patterns to scale across the cohort.

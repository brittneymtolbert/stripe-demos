How it works

Brief in, kit out

A marketer describes the launch. The agent drafts the core message, three audience angles, channel copy sized for each channel, success metrics, and what needs review before it ships.

Edits are the signal

When a marketer approves a kit, the agent measures how much of its copy they kept. Low scores show where the voice guide or prompts need work.

Adoption you can act on

Usage moves each person along the journey, from first try to champion. The agent suggests who to pair, who to coach, and who is ready to demo for their team.
